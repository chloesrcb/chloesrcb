
# Hi 👋, I'm Chloe Serre-Combe

**PhD in Statistics | Lecturer and Researcher (ATER) at the University of Montpellier**

🎓 I completed my PhD at the [University of Montpellier](https://www.umontpellier.fr/), within the [Institut Montpelliérain Alexander Grothendieck (IMAG)](https://imag.umontpellier.fr/) and the [LEMON team at Inria Montpellier](https://team.inria.fr/lemon/).

🔬 My doctoral research focused on:

*Stochastic generators of extreme precipitation and risk assessment of urban flooding at high spatio-temporal resolution.*

My thesis was supervised by [Gwladys Toulemonde](https://imag.umontpellier.fr/~toulemonde/index.html), [Thomas Opitz](https://biosp.mathnum.inrae.fr/homepage-thomas-opitz), and [Nicolas Meyer](https://sites.google.com/view/nicolasmeyer/accueil).

### 🔍 Research interests

- Extreme value statistics
- Stochastic modelling and simulation
- Extreme precipitation and urban flood risk
- Spatio-temporal modelling
- Environmental risk assessment
